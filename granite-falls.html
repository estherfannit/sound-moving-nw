What Happens on Moving Day

No surprises. Here's exactly how your moving day goes.

  1. 1

    Arrival & First Walk-Through

    We show up on time with a clean, fully stocked truck: pads, shrink wrap and tools. Then we walk through each room with you to agree on what's moving, what's staying, what needs extra care and the order we'll load. We confirm your estimate before any work starts.

  2. 2

    Protection

    We pad and shrink-wrap everything, and we mean everything. Our crew brings its own tools to take furniture apart and put it back together.

  3. 3

    Second Walk-Through

    Before we leave, we check every room again so nothing gets left behind, including our tools and your furniture hardware.

  4. 4

    Final Walk-Through

    At your new place, we put every piece exactly where you want it and reassemble anything we took apart. Want something moved a few inches? Just ask. It can make a big difference in how a room feels.

  5. 5

    Completion

    We clean up, load our pads, stop the clock and go over your final total, based on the hours we worked or the minimum. Pay by cash, check, Venmo, Zelle or card. Card and other non-cash payments carry a 4% fee.
