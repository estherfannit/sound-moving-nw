Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. U.S. DOT # 4501511 and WA UTC # HG069611. We carry a $1,000,000 general liability policy plus full cargo coverage.

Are your estimates binding?

No. Estimates are non-binding. Your final invoice is based on actual labor time, with a 2-hour minimum for local moves. If the job finishes early, you pay less.

What valuation protection do you offer?

Under Washington UTC tariffs there are three options: basic protection at no cost (72 cents per pound per article), replacement cost with a $300 deductible ($1.40 per $100 of declared value), or replacement cost with no deductible ($1.85 per $100 of declared value).

Can you move pianos and oversized items?

Yes. We move oversized, odd-sized and heavy furniture, including pianos and safes, with heavy-duty dollies, rigging straps and ramp equipment.

How do you protect items in the rain?

Upholstered furniture is shrink-wrapped inside the home before it goes outdoors. We lay non-slip runners and drop cloths across entryways and hallways.

How are your crews trained?

Every crew member completes at least 24 field shifts before working in a lead role on a client move.
