Terms & Conditions

1. Change of Details Within General Contract / Bill of Lading
Any change in this General Contract or Bill of Lading, quantity of items on your Inventory or Cube sheet, unplanned stops, or a change of address provided by the client to Sound Moving Northwest (SMNW) may affect your final price.
2. Change of Date — Local Moves
If client cancels 14 days or more before move date:
Deposit returned in full — if we cannot accommodate a new date or your circumstances change. If you change your date and we can accommodate, we apply the entire deposit towards the new move date.
If client cancels 13 days or sooner before move date:
Entire deposit NON-REFUNDABLE if we cannot accommodate a new date. If we can accommodate a new date, we apply $100.00 towards your new date and SMNW retains $100.00. If SMNW is able to rebook your cancelled date, we will refund the remaining $100.00 cancellation fee.
3. Change of Date — Long Distance Moves
If client cancels 14 days or more before move date:
$600.00 of the deposit returned — if we cannot accommodate a new date or your circumstances change. If you change your date and we can accommodate, we apply the entire deposit towards the new move date.
If client cancels 13 days or sooner before move date:
Entire deposit NON-REFUNDABLE if we cannot accommodate a new date. If we can accommodate a new date, we apply $750.00 towards the new date and SMNW retains $250.00. If SMNW can rebook your cancelled date, we will refund the remaining $250.00 cancellation fee.
4. Cancellation by SMNW

If SMNW cancels your service, we will refund the full deposit within 3 business days. We reserve the right to cancel without refund and bill for time already incurred under the following circumstances:

  • You are engaged in illegal or dangerous activity, in the opinion of SMNW.
  • The moving services involve an unsanitary environment involving rodents, pests, foul odors, or situations that would jeopardize the health of our crew.
  • You are intoxicated or otherwise impaired in a way that may risk your safety or the safety of SMNW's crew.
  • You engage in any abusive behavior toward our crew — including physical abuse, sexual harassment, verbal abuse, racist or sexist comments, or any other discriminatory behavior.
