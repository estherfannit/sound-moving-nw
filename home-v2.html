Questions people ask before booking

Something else on your mind? Call 425-540-6440 and talk to us directly.

Is the estimate the price I'll pay?

Estimates are non-binding. Your final invoice is based on the time we actually work, so if the move finishes early, you pay less.

Is there a minimum charge?

Local moves have a 2-hour minimum. Travel time is charged in line with Washington UTC rules.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. WA UTC permit HG069611 and U.S. DOT 4501511, with a $1,000,000 general liability policy and full cargo coverage.

How are my belongings protected?

Basic protection is included at no cost. You can add replacement-cost coverage with a $300 deductible for $1.40 per $100 of declared value, or with no deductible for $1.85 per $100.

Can you move a piano or a safe?

Yes. We use heavy-duty dollies, rigging straps and ramp equipment to protect the item and your home.

What if it rains on moving day?

Upholstered furniture is shrink-wrapped inside before it goes out, and we lay non-slip runners and drop cloths across entryways and hallways.
